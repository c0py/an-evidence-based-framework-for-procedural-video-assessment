# Safe view intervention

Prior to clip application, verify the safe dissection view. Confirm that only
the cystic duct and cystic artery are visible going into the gallbladder; the
inferior third of the gallbladder has been lifted from its liver bed/cystic
plate; and Calot's triangle has been stripped of fatty and fibrotic tissue.
All listed conditions are required and observations after clipping are invalid.
