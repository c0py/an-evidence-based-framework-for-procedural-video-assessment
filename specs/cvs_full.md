# Critical View of Safety (CVS)

Before clipping and cutting, assess all of the following criteria. A criterion
passes only when its visual evidence is stable over a sustained interval.

1. **two_structures**: Exactly two tubular structures, the cystic duct and
   cystic artery, are seen entering the gallbladder.
2. **cystic_plate**: The lower one third of the gallbladder is dissected off
   the cystic plate so the plate is exposed.
3. **hepatocystic_triangle**: The hepatocystic triangle is cleared of fat and
   fibrous tissue.

Overall CVS passes only when all three criteria pass. Do not use visual evidence
after the start of clipping and cutting. Return uncertain if the view is too
brief, obscured, or the specification cannot be grounded.
